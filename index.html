Meet the Reef Ecosystem

Discover how every species supports the living pulse of our oceans

🪸

Coral Polyps

The Architects

Tiny animals that secrete calcium carbonate skeletons, creating the structural foundation for 25% of ocean life.

🐢

Sea Turtles

The Gardeners

Grazers that maintain healthy seagrass beds and trim sponges to give corals room to grow.

🐠

Reef Fish

The Cleaners

Herbivorous fish like parrotfish eat smothering algae off coral surfaces, keeping reefs clean.

🌿

Mangroves

The Nursery

Coastal root systems filter land runoff and shelter juvenile marine life before they swim to outer reefs.

🔬

Plankton

The Oxygen Generator

Microscopic organisms generating over 50% of Earth's oxygen and feeding the marine food web.

What kind of promises can I make?

Every promise counts — big or small. Here are a few types of promises and what they actually help with:

🥤

Reduce Plastic Use

e.g. "I will stop using single-use plastic." — Less plastic waste means fewer bottles and straws harming turtles and fish.

🏖️

Cleanup Action

e.g. "I will pick up litter at the beach." — Direct cleanup keeps trash from breaking down into microplastics.

💧

Water & Energy Saving

e.g. "I will save water and electricity." — Reduces emissions that warm the ocean and cause coral bleaching.

🛍️

Responsible Choices

e.g. "I will choose reef-safe sunscreen." — Stops harmful chemicals from washing into fragile ecosystems.

Type your own promise in any of these categories — every action adds up. 🌊
