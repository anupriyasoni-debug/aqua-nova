Why It Matters

Coral reefs support nearly 25% of all marine life, but more than half of the world's reefs have already died or been severely damaged. Pollution, plastic waste, and rising ocean temperatures are destroying homes for fish, turtles, and coral together.

REEF exists to turn awareness into action — connecting people directly to the reefs and marine life they can help protect.
