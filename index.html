Why It Matters

Coral reefs cover less than 1% of the ocean floor, yet they support nearly 25% of all marine species — from tiny reef fish to sea turtles and sharks. They are often called the "rainforests of the sea" because of how much life depends on them.

But today, more than half of the world's coral reefs have already died or been severely damaged. Rising ocean temperatures cause coral bleaching, plastic pollution smothers reef systems, and overfishing disrupts the delicate balance that keeps these ecosystems alive.

The damage isn't limited to reefs alone — when coral dies, the fish that depend on it disappear too, followed by the larger species that feed on those fish. It's a chain reaction that eventually reaches human communities who rely on oceans for food, income, and coastal protection.

REEF exists to break that chain. We connect everyday people directly to real conservation efforts — through volunteering, funding, and awareness — turning passive concern into active protection.

What kind of promises can I make?

Every promise counts — big or small. Here are a few types of promises and what they actually help with:

🥤

Reduce Plastic Use

e.g. "I will stop using single-use plastic." — Less plastic waste means fewer bottles, bags, and straws ending up in the ocean where turtles and fish mistake them for food.

🏖️

Cleanup Action

e.g. "I will pick up litter at the beach." — Direct cleanup keeps trash from reaching the water and breaking down into microplastics that harm coral and marine life.

💧

Water & Energy Saving

e.g. "I will save water and electricity at home." — Lower energy use reduces the emissions that warm the oceans and cause coral bleaching.

🛍️

Responsible Choices

e.g. "I will choose reef-safe sunscreen." — Everyday product choices can stop harmful chemicals from washing into reef ecosystems.

📢

Spread Awareness

e.g. "I will tell my friends why reefs matter." — Awareness turns one promise into many, building a bigger movement to protect marine life.

Type your own promise in any of these categories — or make up your own. Every action, however small, adds up. 🌊
