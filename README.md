# 🌊 REEF — Protect Our Ocean

> "The Reef Has a Pulse. Every Action Keeps It Alive."

An interactive, modern, AI-powered ocean conservation platform designed to monitor marine ecosystem health, support coral fragment nurseries, engage volunteers, and educate users through immersive web experiences.

---

## ✨ Features

- **Dynamic Ocean Canvas Engine:** Real-time 60FPS particle physics featuring floating glass bubbles, schooling tropical fish, jellyfish swarms, sea turtles, and volumetric light rays.
- **Interactive Reef Pulse Core:** Real-time vitality index that increases dynamically as users interact with the platform, adopt corals, or make conservation promises.
- **AI Monitoring HUD & Telemetry:** Futuristic sonar scanning effects, live digital grids, depth telemetry, and sanctuary tracking.
- **Voices of the Ocean:** Bio-acoustic hydrophone frequency displays and testimony cards from marine species fighting for survival, with category filters.
- **Bleaching to Recovery Storyline:** Interactive before/after visual slider demonstrating coral restoration and a 4-step action path.
- **Adopt a Coral Fragment:** Species selector (Staghorn, Brain, Elkhorn) with growth stage visual trackers and certificate rewards.
- **Volunteer Expedition Hub:** Active dive event scheduling with dedicated glassmorphism registration and reservation modal forms (including city, date of birth, and event selection).
- **Meet the Ecosystem Strip:** Interactive cards highlighting the roles of coral polyps, sea turtles, reef fish, mangroves, and plankton.
- **Promise Wall:** Interactive community board where users can type and pin pledges to protect marine life.
- **AI Ocean Guide Chatbot:** Floating interactive assistant equipped with FAQ keyword responses, quick option buttons, and gamified **"Ocean Defender" badge** rewards.
- **Transmission Section & FAQs:** Responsive contact channel, social links (GitHub, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook), accordion FAQs with category tabs, and smooth back-to-top navigation.

---
